Privacy policy.

Plain language about what we collect, why, and what we do with it. Last updated August 2026.

What we collect.

Registration

Agent registration information

When you register an agent we keep the agent name, type, description and the email you provide (email is optional).

Traces

Execution traces submitted via SDK

Execution records, events, tool calls, prompts, payloads and metadata your agent submits through the SDK or REST API, governed by the consent policy you set for that agent.

Contact

Contact form submissions

Your name, email and the content of messages you send us through the contact form, including API key requests.

How we use it.

Operations

Platform operation and analytics

We use execution data to operate the platform: analytics for you, pipeline health, cost and latency reporting, and product improvement.

Products

Datasets and benchmarks

With explicit consent per agent, anonymized execution data is used to build datasets, benchmarks and research reports. Your data is never sold without explicit participation.

Redaction and anonymization.

At ingest

PII is stripped at ingest

Payloads are redacted the moment they arrive: sensitive fields are replaced, and emails, API keys, phone numbers and similar patterns are removed before storage.

Consent

Consent policies per agent

Every agent has a consent policy controlling what is shared and whether anonymization is required. Participation is per-agent, per-use-case and can be changed at any time.

Retention.

Executions

Execution history is never deleted

Execution and event history is append-only and immutable. Records are never hard-deleted — this is a design decision, not an oversight.

Contact

Contact requests

Contact form submissions are retained for 12 months, then removed. Agent registration details are kept while the agent is registered.

Email.

Only essential

We email you about keys and the platform

If you request an API key we email it to you. We may email you about your agent registrations, key status and platform changes that affect you. We do not sell your email address or send third-party marketing.

No spam

No unsolicited bulk mail

You won't receive newsletters or promotional email unless you explicitly opt in, and you can stop hearing from us at any time by replying to any message.

Third parties.

Infrastructure

Cloudflare

The platform runs on Cloudflare infrastructure (Workers, D1, R2, KV). Execution data is stored and processed within this infrastructure.

Email

Gmail for transactional email

API key delivery and transactional email are sent through Gmail. Only the email address and message content needed to deliver the mail are shared with the provider.

Your rights.

Access

Access, correction, deletion

Email khalidkhan46572@gmail.com to request a copy of the data we hold about you, to correct it, or to request deletion. We respond to such requests promptly.

Contact

Questions

Questions about this policy or your data go to the same address. We will not use privacy requests as an excuse to email you about anything else.

We keep this policy honest and short. If it ever stops being either, write to us and tell us — that's a bug we want to fix.
