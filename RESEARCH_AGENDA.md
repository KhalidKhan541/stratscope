# Purpose

This document defines the long-term scientific and engineering questions that guide StratScope.

Products change.

Markets change.

Models change.

Infrastructure changes.

Research questions remain.

Every major feature should exist because it helps answer one or more questions in this document.

If a feature does not contribute to our research agenda or product mission, it should be questioned before implementation.

---

# Our Mission

Create the scientific and engineering foundations of Execution Intelligence.

Just as:

Operating Systems standardized process management,

Git standardized software history,

Kubernetes standardized container orchestration,

OpenTelemetry standardized observability,

StratScope aims to standardize Execution Intelligence for AI systems.

---

# Definition

Execution Intelligence is the discipline of capturing, understanding, evaluating, improving, governing, and learning from AI executions.

An execution is treated as a durable organizational asset rather than a temporary computation.

---

# Long-Term Vision

We believe future AI systems will require infrastructure capable of:

Understanding what happened.

Explaining why it happened.

Learning from historical executions.

Predicting future failures.

Optimizing future executions.

Sharing organizational intelligence.

Operating safely at enterprise scale.

The objective of StratScope Research is to make these capabilities measurable, reproducible, and standardized.

# Research Themes

Our work is organized into six major research areas.

1. Execution Representation
2. Evaluation Science
3. Organizational Learning
4. Autonomous Optimization
5. Governance & Trust
6. Open Standards

Each area contains foundational research questions.

---

# Research Area 1

Execution Representation

Question

What is the best representation of an AI execution?

Today's systems represent:

Chats

Prompts

Messages

Logs

Tasks

We believe the execution itself should become the primary computational object.

Research Questions

Can every AI framework map to one canonical execution model?

How should execution lineage be represented?

How should nested agent executions be modeled?

How should execution graphs evolve?

Can execution replay become deterministic?

Desired Outcome

The Execution Specification (EXS) becomes a widely adopted standard.

---

# Research Area 2

Evaluation Science

Question

How should AI executions be evaluated?

Current evaluation often focuses on benchmarks.

Real production systems require richer measurements.

Research Questions

How should success be measured?

How should cost efficiency be measured?

Can execution quality be predicted before completion?

Can confidence be calibrated?

How should hallucinations be quantified?

Can evaluation become domain independent?

Desired Outcome

A standardized execution evaluation framework suitable for enterprise AI.

---

# Research Area 3

Reflection & Organizational Learning

Question

How should organizations learn from AI?

Today's AI forgets.

Organizations should not.

Research Questions

How should reflections be structured?

What information should become reusable knowledge?

How can repeated failures become organizational memory?

How should conflicting knowledge be resolved?

How should knowledge evolve over time?

Desired Outcome

A practical framework for persistent organizational AI memory.

---

# Research Area 4

Autonomous Optimization

Question

Can AI systems improve themselves responsibly?

Research Questions

When should optimization occur?

What should never be optimized automatically?

How should optimization confidence be measured?

Can execution history predict future improvements?

Can recommendations be ranked by expected business value?

Desired Outcome

Explainable optimization rather than opaque automation.

---

# Research Area 5

Governance & Trust

Question

How should enterprise AI remain trustworthy?

Research Questions

How should AI decisions be audited?

How should execution provenance be represented?

How should policy violations be detected?

Can governance become real-time?

How should regulations integrate with execution intelligence?

Desired Outcome

Execution Intelligence becomes a governance layer for enterprise AI.

---

# Research Area 6

Open Standards

Question

Can Execution Intelligence become an industry standard?

Research Questions

Should Execution Intelligence Protocol (EIP) become an open specification?

How should SDKs interoperate?

Should execution artifacts become portable?

Can multiple vendors share execution intelligence?

How should community governance work?

Desired Outcome

Execution Intelligence becomes an ecosystem rather than a proprietary product.

---

# Research Area 7

Execution Intelligence Datasets

Question

What constitutes a high-quality execution intelligence dataset?

Research Questions

- How should execution records be curated for research use?
- What filtering criteria produce the most valuable datasets?
- How should dataset quality be measured?
- Can dataset quality be automatically validated?
- How should dataset versioning ensure reproducibility?

Desired Outcome

A standardized methodology for generating production-grade execution intelligence datasets.

# Research Area 8

Benchmark Methodology

Question

How should AI execution benchmarks be designed?

Research Questions

- What metrics best capture execution quality?
- How should benchmarks account for different domains?
- Can benchmarks be automatically updated as new executions arrive?
- How should benchmark confidence be measured?
- What sample sizes are statistically valid?

Desired Outcome

A benchmark framework that provides reliable, reproducible comparisons of AI execution quality.

# Research Area 9

Research Corpus Design

Question

How should execution intelligence be organized for research consumption?

Research Questions

- What constitutes a well-curated research corpus?
- How should corpora balance diversity and specificity?
- Can corpus quality be automatically assessed?
- How should corpora evolve as new execution data arrives?

Desired Outcome

A corpus design methodology that maximizes research utility while maintaining data quality.
